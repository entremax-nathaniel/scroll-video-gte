This is what a scroll-caption video looks like.
Drop one photo, one music track and one text file into a folder, and the tool turns them into a vertical video.
Every sentence becomes its own block, and long sentences break where a person reading aloud would pause, because that is how these captions are meant to be read.
The music starts at the very first frame, and if the script runs longer than the track, the track repeats with a soft crossfade.
That is the whole tool. Edit the script, swap the photo, pick a different song, and render again.
